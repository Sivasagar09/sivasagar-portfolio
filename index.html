Auction It Out

An IPL-style player auction for your group.

Open the app Try the solo demo
React · Supabase realtime · Vercel

Friends sign in, join an auction with a code and bid live against a timer, building a squad within their purse. After the auction, the app scores each squad from real match scorecards, match by match or across a series.

  • Live bidding room with a voice announcer and a video lounge
  • Fantasy points from a pasted scorecard, with captain and vice-captain picks
  • Leaderboard you can share on WhatsApp
Product decisions
  • Started as a single-player prototype against simulated teams to test the feel, then rebuilt as a live multiplayer app after people loved it.
  • Every bid and sale goes through checks on the server, so nobody can change scores or purses from their browser.
  • Ships in small, frequent releases: captain picks, an upcoming-players view, video in the room.

Plot to Plan

Sketch your house, floor by floor.

Try the prototype

A drafting tool for people building an independent house. Enter the plot, lay out rooms to exact sizes and see the result in 2D and 3D. Architects and contractors use it to agree a draft with the homeowner before the approval drawings.

  • Rooms, doors, columns and a staircase sized automatically to the floor height
  • Structural checks, like rooms stacking and bathrooms lining up across floors
  • Optional Vastu hints, and a 3D walkthrough of the house
Product decisions
  • Scoped to G+1 and G+2 houses and villas first; apartments and commercial buildings come later.
  • A draft for agreement, not an approved plan: the architect still signs off, and the app says so.
  • Free rough sketches for homeowners; architects would pay per project.
