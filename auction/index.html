Setup
Before the auction · Agree the rules with all participants, load the player sheet, then start. Rules lock once bidding begins.

Rules

The clock restarts after every bid. When it runs out, the player goes to the highest bidder.
Bid increments Each click adds a fixed amount. Once the bid reaches a level's starting price, that level's raise applies. Amounts in ₹ lakh (100 = ₹1 Cr).
Squad rules
Teams can't spend or fill slots in a way that leaves them unable to meet these rules.
